## How we use the handbook (rules from its own header)

- Gemini did **not** watch the show: scene descriptions, quotes, lore details and engagement numbers are leads to verify against primary material before a video depends on them.
- Keep four layers apart: Slugterra canon, fan theory and fandom, transferable principles, and our original Slugshot invention. Only principles transfer; never names, designs, lore, dialogue, music, footage or branding.
- Fan examples aren't representative samples. English and Spanish audiences are treated as separate cultures; claims about either are hypotheses until our own account data says otherwise.
- Possible anti-AI-art sentiment is a trust hypothesis: never claim something is fully human-made if AI helped.
- **Floppy corrections:** the handbook repeatedly calls Floppy "zero-power" or "harmless". The game says otherwise: Floppy deals 7 damage, no knockback, no transformation, no abilities. Never write that he does nothing, and never give him a secret power reveal.
- After this pass: produce, review, publish, measure, learn. Research and engineering only answer a concrete production problem or observed result.

## Open owner decision flagged by Claude (2026-11-25)

Most of the game's slug species names match Slugterra species names (Rammstone, Tormato, Phosphoro, Aquabeek, Boon Doc, Frostcrawler, Arachnet, Tazerling, Vinedrill, Flatulorhinkus, Hop Rock, Frightgeist), and the game docs call Inferno's dragon form "Burpy" (Slugterra's lead slug). Saying or showing these names publicly risks trademark or copyright problems and an implied affiliation, which the handbook's own rules prohibit. Marketing scripts avoid speaking species names until the owner decides; the game itself is untouched.
