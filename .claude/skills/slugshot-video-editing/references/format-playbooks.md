# Format playbooks

The research PDF's starting mix for six daily edits is a hypothesis, not an optimum: 2 gameplay/PvP, 1 story, 1 character/dance, 1 choice/meme, 1 promo or experiment.

## PvP gameplay (preset PVP): "prove the game is fun"

- Real footage from a verified build only. **(A: truth; research PDF)**
- Open on the most legible moment that states the stakes (mismatch, bad position, last shot), then rewind if needed ("10 seconds earlier..."). **(C/D)**
- Hook families from the research: underdog mismatch ("He brought Inferno. I brought Floppy."), impossible-looking state ("No way I survive this shot."), dilemma ("Shoot him or break line of sight?"). **(D: to be tested)**
- One punch-in on the decisive hit. Crop unless the HUD or the whole arena tells the story.
- End within about a second of the payoff, then the end card.

## Direct promo / ad (preset PROMO): "convert / learn"

- Answer in seconds: what is it, what makes it different, what can I do in it. **(research PDF)**
- One concrete moment beats a feature list ("14 slugs, abilities, maps..." is out). **(research PDF)**
- Pre-launch wording: "in development", follow or wishlist-style asks. No "play now". **(A: game truth)**
- Disclose commercial content on TikTok. **(A)**

## Fiction: In My World / In This Town (preset WORLD): "build attachment"

- Rule -> violation -> consequence -> twist. State the rule in the first line. **(research PDF; C: reference clips)**
- Six to ten visual beats at most; stills with slow push are fine. **(research PDF)**
- The "Fictional story" label stays on; canon stays canon (Floppy never transforms, even in a dream, unless clearly a fantasy and labelled). **(A/research PDF)**
- Settle the story with real gameplay where possible ("settle it with a duel"), clearly marked as the real part. **(D)**

## Character, dance and skit (preset SKIT): "build slug recognition"

- The slug's personality must change the joke (Inferno powers up on the beat; Floppy keeps the same dance). **(research PDF)**
- Comedy timing rules apply: setup, pause, punch, cut. **(D)**
- Character consistency over spectacle; no new designs. **(research PDF)**

## Memes and trends (preset SKIT or PVP): "participation"

- Copy the format grammar (audio cue + roles + timing + social meaning + reveal), never the edit itself. **(research PDF)**
- Only when it connects naturally to Slugshot. Track the trend's state (emerging / active / saturated) and skip saturated ones. **(C/D)**
- Platform audio is added in the app.

## Cinematic / AI scenes from Flow (preset AISCENE): "shots too costly to animate"

- Generated from approved reference stills (UK: no uploaded-video editing in Flow). **(A: Flow regional limits)**
- Review frame by frame against references: silhouette, proportions, face, colours. Any drift = reject. **(research PDF)**
- Always labelled; never intercut with gameplay as if it happened. **(A: disclosure; research PDF)**
- Short (3-8 s) shots carried by narration; let one strong frame breathe rather than stacking many weak ones. **(D)**

## Choice / comment (preset CHOICE): "participation -> next video"

- Freeze at a real decision point, ask, show one real result; if the comments overwhelmingly choose the other, make that the reply video. **(research PDF; A: YouTube encourages reply content)**
- The end card is the question; keep it to one line.
