# Visual direction: an original creature-adventure look

What makes a Slugshot video recognisably ours instead of another interchangeable "In My World" Roblox story. Built from the game's confirmed art direction (`../slugshot/docs/art/style-guide.md`, "Living Comic-Book Fantasy") and the first real frames (2026-11-25). Evidence labels as in SKILL.md; everything here is **D** until real posts say otherwise. Owner approval pending.

## The identity in one line

**An underground world of glowing caverns and characterful creatures, inked like a comic and directed like an animated adventure series**, with Roblox comedy where the joke wants it.

Slugterra is a *feeling* we share, not a source:
- companionship with creatures;
- caverns that glow;
- mysteries and lore;
- tactical duels;
- expressive animated acting.

Take none of its creatures, names, designs, slinger/blaster look, sounds, music, dialogue or logo. Imply no affiliation.

Transferable principles from the owner's Slugterra handbook (`docs/creative/README.md`; principles only, never names or lore):
- the creature is a companion with opinions, not just ammunition;
- tactile, fallible gear and caverns that feel lived in;
- sincere stakes sitting beside petty, irreverent comedy;
- every creature stays relevant through attitude and tactics rather than raw power;
- wordless creature-only stories travel across languages.

## What we already have (use it)

- **Real sets with depth.** The lobby's portals (orange, green, violet) frame a shot with a built-in colour story. The mushroom canopy and the inked rock give foreground and background layers. Shoot there before building anything new.
- **Characters with built-in personality.**
  - Floppy: drowsy and deadpan, glowing ringed eyes.
  - Rammstone: armoured swagger, glowing eyes, a grin that turns to a grimace.
  - Aquabeek: cool, half-lidded.
  - Tormato: spinning storm eyes.
  - Boon Doc: halo and wings.
  - Phosphoro: light.
  - Inferno: fire.
- **The ink.** Charcoal outlines on characters (the runner adds them), heavier ink on rock. It is the signature: keep it in every look.
- **One font.** Kalam Bold, the game's UI font (owner, 2026-11-22), in all on-screen text with a charcoal ink edge.

## Looks (presets to choose per scene, not one filter for everything)

| Look | When | Camera | Light and colour | Edit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grove comedy** (default) | skits, banter, meme beats | eye-level close-ups, over-the-shoulder, a held two-character frame | the lobby as it is: calm cool base, portal glow as accent | cuts on reactions, held silences, speaker-coloured captions |
| **Living comic lore** | "what if", lore, Potential Man-style powerscaling | slow push-ins, low hero angles (`slug_low_angle`), orbit reveals | darker base (`look.lighting`: lower Brightness/Ambient), one strong accent colour per beat | dossier cards, manga-panel splits, a narrator voice |
| **Anime impact** (rare, labelled fiction) | an exaggerated beat that is clearly fantasy | snap zoom, whip, held freeze | high contrast, one colour | 1-4 s; original stylised eyes and aura only; never a copied technique; never shown as gameplay |
| **Authentic gameplay** | real PvP clips | the player's own camera or a clean spectator angle | untouched | minimal: punch-ins and captions only; no staged effects mixed in |

## Composition rules (vertical)

- Each shot answers one question: who matters now? Frame that character big (`close_up` size 1.0-1.3), eyes in the upper-middle, captions in the lower third.
- Depth staging (near and far) instead of side-by-side; a side two-shot doesn't fit 9:16.
- Colour hierarchy (style guide):
  - about 70% calm base, 20% secondary, 10% accent;
  - one glowing accent behind a subject is plenty;
  - if everything glows, nothing does.
- Silhouettes read at phone size: check a still at 25% scale.
- Restraint:
  - no gratuitous screen shake or heavy filters;
  - no transitions that don't mean something (a whip pan only between two characters or two places).

## How to judge a frame

1. Readable at phone size? Who's the subject?
2. Does it look like *our* world (ink, cavern glow, our creatures) and not a default Roblox baseplate?
3. Is anything clipping the frame edge by accident?
4. Is the label visible, and no game name shown while the reveal lock is on?
