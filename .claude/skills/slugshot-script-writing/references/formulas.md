# Formula routes (TEST, from Gemini; built from post-hoc clusters, not validated)

Use one as a starting template, then follow the confirmed rules. Each route lists Gemini's suggested length. Treat lengths as starting points.

1. **Cultural-meme sprint (45–75 s).** An active meme or everyday taboo flipped mid-video. Rule plus cue in one breath, three escalating townsfolk examples (rule of three), the narrator's escape, then an ironic reversal and a clean button with no CTA. Example in the data: "In this town everybody huss… except me" (19.9M). Risk: late copies of a meme flop (a copy of the same template got 6.5k). Flip it, don't repeat it.
2. **Deadpan civic-absurdity loop (55–85 s or 115–145 s).** An everyday annoyance turned into a town law, told dead straight, with a loophole or self-incrimination twist. Examples: one-minute showers, sunlight subscriptions, banned letters. *Deadpan as a divider did not replicate blind; the structure is still a good template.*
3. **Rule-horror cliffhanger (80–150 s).** A rule, its fatal consequence, the narrator breaks it, sensory dread, a false relief, then the safe thing turns out to be unsafe. End either resolved with a chilling stinger or on a cliffhanger with a real outlet ("Part 2 tomorrow" or a two-option choice).
4. **Emotional recontextualisation fable (125–170 s).** A poetic one-sentence rule, early small laughs, a planted detail, a crisis, then a reveal that the feared or embarrassing thing was love or sacrifice. Needs Act-1 wit, or it reads as uniformly sombre.
5. **Fourth-wall platform meta (95–130 s).** Platform behaviour as town physics ("your screen time controls the weather"). This was breaking out fast in the under-5-day posts. Avoid coercive bait.
6. **Social-economy dilemma (55–98 s).** One visible metric runs status ("the more friends you have, the lighter you are"). Show the unfairness to a specific person, then end on a moral-choice question or a karmic rebound.
