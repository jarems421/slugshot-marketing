## "Wrong answer, get him" (owner example, 26 Nov 2026; 2 owner examples and 45 search posts; OBSERVATIONS)

- **Owner example** (@jaydengzz04, 10 s, 2.5M views, 51k shares):
  1. a selfie at school;
  2. a quick question ("Yo, who's your…?");
  3. a wrong answer, hesitant ("Uh, …?");
  4. a one-line signal plus a hand wave;
  5. **hard cut** to black and white as the crew moves in through the crowd.

  The audio is one loud 144 BPM hype beat the whole way through (about −9 dB). Its bass peaks on the wrong answer and the signal (2–4 s), so the edit lands on the drop. The original language has slurs and swearing; only the structure is usable.
- **The family is wider than one sound.** "Question, wrong answer, consequence" is an evergreen skit grammar:
  - the dog treat bait (11.9M);
  - "favourite snack?" / "Um, Cheez-It" (4.8M);
  - classroom wrong-answer punishments (4.8M, 680k);
  - "favourite rapper?" variants (6.7M in 2023; 513k in Oct 2026).
- **The school "get him" crew clip is live.** The top search posts are from Aug–Nov 2026 (3.6M, 2.0M, 1.7M), with a median of 628k. Most have no speech at all: the sound and the cut carry the joke.
- **Why it works (JUDGEMENT):**
  - the viewer knows the right answer before the character speaks;
  - the pause before the wrong answer is the setup;
  - the drop and the colour change are the punchline, with no explanation.

  It's short: 5–10 s in the originals.
- **For Slugshot (TEST):**
  - As a **mid-video cut** (owner idea): inside a longer story, at the turn, a character gives the wrong answer. Freeze, then the beat drops, and the edit cuts to black-and-white slow motion as the crew walks in (the slug-shot cutscene engine already has slow motion and split screen).
  - Example: Rammstone asks, "Who's the strongest slug in the cavern?" Floppy, hesitantly: "…Me?" Signal line: "Lads." The crew drops in. Then cut straight back to the story.
  - Clean language only. The consequence stays cartoon-silly (a chase, a bonk, a dramatic walk), never a real beating.
  - **Sound:** never bake the original sound into the master. Either add a trending sound in the app on a version cut for it (audio-design rule), or use our own drop: `riser` into `sub_boom` on the signal, then M5 arena-hype. It needs the gap: the bed cuts out for the pause before the wrong answer.
  - The question must be one the viewer can answer instantly from the story so far, or the wrong answer won't read.

## "I wasn't born yet" aura edits (owner example, 26 Nov 2026; OBSERVATIONS)

- **Owner example** (@okokx0944, 15 s, 62k views):
  - a classical-painting aura edit, Michael versus Lucifer;
  - slow kinetic subtitles;
  - a quiet, sparse bed, almost no bass (−27 to −42 dB);
  - a JJK line said dead serious: "The only reason you were the strongest sorcerer in history… simply because… I wasn't born yet."

  The humour is the **crossover played completely straight**: an anime boast moved onto the wrong subject.
- **The line is a live template.** Fifteen search posts from 7 Aug to 6 Nov 2026 all use the same line on a swapped subject: Gojo (954k, the earliest seen), then Spider-Man, Blue Lock, the Flash, Voldemort, Princess Yue. The median is 25k: still in use, past its peak. The line stays the same and only the subject changes.
- **Archangel aura edits are an evergreen niche** (2025–2026, median 595k): serious, faith-coded, not jokes. That's the setting the owner's example plays against, not a meme in itself.
- **Why it works (JUDGEMENT):** total sincerity, slow pacing, the gap between the grandeur of the visuals and where the line comes from. Viewers need to know the line; the swapped subject is the joke.
- **For Slugshot (TEST):**
  - Floppy painted as a classical hero or saint (an inked "oil painting" frame, glowing cavern light), a slow push-in, captions word by word: "The only reason you were the strongest slug in the cavern… is simply because… I hadn't hatched yet." Say Floppy's 7 damage on screen only if the joke is that it's tiny; never claim game power he doesn't have.
  - Keep it **parody of the edit style, not of a faith**: use painting-style framing and light, never real religious figures, scripture or icons. Kids-to-18 audience.
  - Quote at most a short line from the anime; the speaker and visuals are ours. Recheck the line's status before posting; it's already past its peak.
  - Sound: quiet, slow and serious (M3 warm-glow or M4 glow-dread under it, low), no stings until a single `sparkle_reveal` or `sub_boom` on "hatched".
