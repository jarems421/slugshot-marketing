# Decision memo (end of every standard or consequential study)

Generated from the ledger's `decision_memo` by `ss evidence card <ID>`; write it in this shape.

```
DECISION              what we recommend (one or two sentences)
SUPPORTED FINDINGS    claim IDs only
UNCERTAIN BUT USEFUL  claim IDs + why still uncertain
COUNTEREVIDENCE       strongest opposing evidence
WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW
CREATIVE HYPOTHESIS   If [mechanism] is true, then [Slugshot concept] should improve [our own metric] because [reason].
CHEAPEST TEST         variant / control / primary metric / observation window / result that would change our mind
```

Suggestive evidence can justify a cheap test; it never becomes "this causes virality".
